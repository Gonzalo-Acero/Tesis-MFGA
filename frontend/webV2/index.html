Must-See Argentine Landmarks

El Cabildo

The historic government house of colonial Buenos Aires, now a national museum.

Caminito

The colorful street museum in La Boca, birthplace of tango culture.

El Obelisco

Buenos Aires' iconic 67m-tall monument celebrating the city's founding.

La Feria de Mataderos

Traditional fair showcasing gaucho culture, folk music, and artisan crafts.

Iguazu Falls

One of the world's most spectacular waterfalls at the border with Brazil.

Perito Moreno Glacier

The stunning advancing glacier in Los Glaciares National Park.
